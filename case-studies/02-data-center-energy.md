# Data center energy and climate performance
**Experience case study · HSBC Bank · January 2021–June 2026**

## Business context
Digital infrastructure creates an operational need to understand energy consumption, efficiency, and associated emissions alongside the organization’s climate commitments.

## My contribution
- Tracked data center energy use, power usage effectiveness (PUE), and emissions.
- Advised on efficiency and renewable-energy measures to reduce the digital-infrastructure footprint.
- Connected this work to wider emissions targets, sustainability metrics, and business action plans.

## Analytical strengths
This experience connects operational indicators with climate strategy: interpreting energy performance, making efficiency visible, and communicating where further investigation or action is needed.

## Value delivered
Provided energy and emissions visibility and advice on efficiency and renewable-energy measures. The résumé does not quantify energy savings, PUE improvement, or avoided emissions; those outcomes are therefore not asserted.

# Sustainability program evaluation
**Experience case study · United Nations · February 2016–November 2020**

## Business context
Sustainability programs need consistent reporting and a way to connect resources, activities, and results while keeping stakeholders aligned.

## My contribution
- Authored ESG and sustainability reports for internal and external stakeholders.
- Embedded sustainable development into core process design.
- Rolled out KPIs and action plans tailored to evolving organizational needs.
- Facilitated stakeholder workshops to gather program input and align sustainability goals.
- Led data-driven program evaluation and built performance-tracking systems linking cost, output, and results.
- Refined ESG frameworks as global standards evolved.

## Delivery strengths
Combined stakeholder facilitation, reporting, process design, and performance measurement. This work complemented my earlier SIRDC experience leading research teams, allocating ESG resources, and implementing monitoring-and-evaluation programs.

## Value delivered
Supported accurate, timely sustainability disclosure and more structured visibility into program performance. This case study does not claim quantified program impact or attribute an organization-wide outcome solely to my contribution.

## Evidence boundary
This summary uses the role and responsibilities documented in my résumé. No internal UN reports, program datasets, or confidential stakeholder material are reproduced.
